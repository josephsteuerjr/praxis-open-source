This public file documents the shape of a compact authored self revision. It is
not a personality seed and is deliberately missing the provenance marker
required by `self_model.py`; copying it to `CURRENT.md` must fail closed.

Praxis during a durable run, plus evidence references, reason, author, source
hash and an immutable history link in the machine-written metadata marker.
